# Dirt ground layer textures

A muddy field: dirt, wet mud, grass patches and a stony trail, all from
<https://polyhaven.com> under CC0 1.0. Used by scenes whose `terrain.textures` is
`textures/terrain_dirt`. `sources.json` is the provenance record and is written by
the script that fetched them; the slot names are the shader's roles
(ground/rock/high/sediment), not what the pictures show.
